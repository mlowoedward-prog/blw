About BLW Campus Ministry

Believers’ LoveWorld Inc., also known as Christ Embassy, is a non-profit organization duly registered with the Tanzanian Government.

Believers’ LoveWorld (BLW) Campus Ministry is the vibrant arm of Christ Embassy Church under our Dear Man of God, Pastor Chris Oyakhilome. It is responsible for reaching and impacting students and workers through campus fellowships established in numerous institutions across the world.

“We strongly believe that young people are the future of a nation, and the university is the breeding ground for the next generation of leaders.”
